# Privacy notice — "Recall" object-finding study

*Version 1.0 (2026-10-25). Give this to participants before consent. Fill in the bracketed fields.*

**Who is responsible for your data:** [researcher name, affiliation, email]. [If applicable: the institution's
data-protection officer: contact.]

**Why we collect it:** to measure whether a phone assistant helps people find misplaced objects faster and with
less effort than searching on their own or with a simpler assistant. [Legal basis, if you are in the UK/EU:
your consent / the institution's public-task research basis — as specified by your ethics approval.]

## What we collect, where it is kept, and for how long

| data | where | how long |
|---|---|---|
| Chest-camera video of the ~15-min tidy-up phase, and the assistants' memory index built from it | Study phone only; offline (airplane mode, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth off) for the whole session | **Deleted at the end of your session, in front of you** |
| Search times, found/not found, assistants' answers and displayed confidence | Paper run sheet, then a spreadsheet file under your code (e.g. P07) on the researcher's encrypted laptop | Paper: shredded after it has been typed up and checked (within 7 days). File: [5 years / per institution policy] |
| Questionnaire answers (effort, ease of use, trust) | Paper, then the same coded file | Same as above |
| Age band (e.g. 25–34) and smartphone-familiarity rating | The same coded file | Same as above |
| Your name and signature | Consent form + link sheet (paper only), in a locked folder | Link sheet: destroyed on [withdrawal deadline]. Consent form: [per institution policy, e.g. 5 years] |

**Nothing leaves the device**: the video is never uploaded, copied, sent to a cloud service, or used to train
any model. The assistants run entirely on the study phone. The coded data file is never put on cloud storage or
email; only aggregate statistics (averages, test results — never individual rows) are published.

## Who can see your data

Only the research team named above. Aggregate results may appear in a paper or a public code repository.

## Your rights

You can stop the session at any time (your video is deleted immediately and your measurements are discarded).
Until [withdrawal deadline] you can ask for your coded data to be deleted by emailing [researcher email] — after
that date we can no longer tell which code is yours, so the data are anonymous. You can ask what data we hold
about you and complain to [ethics committee / data-protection authority contact].
